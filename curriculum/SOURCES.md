## Authority and limitations

- Issuing-organization documents control certification scope. This repository's maps are navigation aids, not substitutes.
- The project mirror contained no uploaded source documents at execution time. The official sources above were therefore used and the exact versions recorded.
- RCWA covers the full RUCKUS portfolio. This home lab provides real Unleashed, R650, H350, and ICX practice but cannot prove SmartZone, RUCKUS One, Cloudpath, controller clustering, data planes, licensing, API, large-scale design, or enterprise AAA experience. Those rows are marked **Study/simulate**.
- Product behavior varies by model and software release. Record model, sanitized version, and date in every device-specific lab.
- Exam blueprints can change. Review this register before scheduling an exam and whenever a source link or version changes.

## Ethical preparation

Use official objectives, legitimate training, original practice questions, and documented hands-on work. Do not add recalled live-exam questions or unauthorized “brain dump” material to this repository.
